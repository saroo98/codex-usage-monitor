# Code of Conduct

## Expected conduct

Participants must communicate respectfully, focus on technical merit, accept constructive review, protect user privacy, and avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, or deliberate disruption.

## Unacceptable conduct

Unacceptable behavior includes personal attacks, publishing private information, sexualized or discriminatory language, sustained disruption, impersonation, malicious contributions, and attempts to introduce secrets, malware, or misleading release artifacts.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide, reject, or remove comments, issues, pull requests, or contributions that violate this policy. Repeated or severe violations may result in temporary or permanent restrictions.

## Reporting

For ordinary conduct concerns, contact the maintainer through a GitHub issue that contains no sensitive personal information. Security-sensitive matters must follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
